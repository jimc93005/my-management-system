The problem

Manual processing & data loss

Current school data management relies heavily on manual data entry, calculations, and physical documentation. This process is time-consuming and inefficient, requiring substantial physical storage space and often resulting in the accumulation of paper-based records.

Our solution

Centralized digital architecture

Our system digitizes and centralizes the management of school data. It automatically performs calculations and generates student performance reports, reducing manual workload, minimizing errors, and eliminating the dependence on physical paperwork.
